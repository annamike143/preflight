## Purpose
This file is the repo-local Phase 11 red-team review record for governed priority risk classes and focus areas.
It now records the W04 scenario-by-scenario evidence review without declaring release readiness on its own.

| Critical | RISK-063 | threatens product truth, secrets, or architectural integrity |
| High | RISK-064 | threatens boundedness, usability, or trust |

| --- | --- |
| blocker candidate | likely release-blocking unless disproven by evidence |
| rework candidate | likely requires bounded corrective work before release-ready |
| residual-risk candidate | may remain only with explicit release accounting and no critical acceptance conflict |
