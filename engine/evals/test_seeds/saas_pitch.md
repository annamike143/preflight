# SaaS Pitch: CloudSync Pro for Healthcare

## Executive Summary
CloudSync Pro is an automated patient records synchronization engine connecting hospital Electronic Health Record (EHR) platforms with outpatient specialty clinics. 

## The Problem
Specialty clinics (cardiology, oncology, orthopedics) spend an average of 4.2 hours per provider per week manually faxing, scanning, and re-entering lab results and imaging reports from regional hospital networks. This creates diagnostic delays, medical error exposure, and administrative burnout.

## The Solution
An intelligent, zero-install HL7/FHIR pipeline that automatically synchronizes, normalizes, and embeds hospital records directly into clinic EHRs within 90 seconds of release.

## Business Model & Economics
- **Pricing:** $499/month per clinic (unlimited providers, up to 5,000 monthly transactions).
- **Customer Acquisition:** Direct sales via medical association conferences and EHR marketplace listings.
- **Estimated CAC:** $3,200. Estimated LTV: $24,000 (churn estimated at < 0.5% monthly).

## Key Assumptions to Validate
1. Hospital IT departments will grant read-only FHIR API access without requiring 9-month custom integration audits.
2. Independent clinics are willing to switch from traditional secure fax workflows to an automated cloud bridge.
3. HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) signing can be standardized through click-through legal agreements.
